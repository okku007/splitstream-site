FAQs

frequently asked

What is SplitStream?

A Formula 1 companion for your home screen: session countdowns, championship standings, your favourite drivers, lap records and a fact a day, always current, so you rarely need to open the app.

Which platforms is it on?

Android, on Google Play. An iOS version built on the same shared core is in development.

Does it cost anything?

No. Free, ad free forever.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, find SplitStream and drag one into place. Some widgets open a short setup for theme or driver.

A widget isn't updating.

Open the app once so it can sync, and check that battery optimisation isn't fully restricting it. Widgets refresh as often as every 30 seconds during a live session and less often otherwise. Still stuck? Email the device model and which widget.

Where does the data come from?

Schedule, standings and results come from the Jolpica / Ergast-compatible API, with session times checked against the official Formula 1 event tracker. SplitStream is not affiliated with Formula 1, FOM, or the FIA.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Email app@technowolf.in with what you saw. For bugs, add your device model and Android version.
