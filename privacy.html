Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

SplitStream ("the app", "we") is an app for tracking Formula 1 schedules, standings, results, and race statistics. This policy covers the Android and iOS apps and their home-screen widgets, and explains what data they handle.

SplitStream does not collect personal data or require user accounts. It contains no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The only usage and crash statistics we see are the aggregated ones the app stores provide, described below.

Disclaimer: SplitStream is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Formula 1, Formula One Licensing BV, FOM, or the FIA. F1, Formula 1, and team/driver names are trademarks of their respective owners.

Data we collect

The app itself sends us nothing. It has no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising code, and it never assigns or transmits a device identifier.

Statistics from the app stores

Google Play (Android vitals) and Apple (App Store Connect analytics and crash reports) give developers aggregated statistics such as install counts, crash rates, and crash logs. They collect this only from people who have agreed, in their device settings, to share diagnostics with app developers, and you can turn that sharing off there at any time. We use these statistics only to find and fix problems. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies govern how they collect it.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • A cache of public Formula 1 data (schedule, standings, results, lap records, trivia).
  • Your app settings (theme, time format, favorite drivers, per-widget theme).
  • On iOS, the widgets read this cache and your widget settings from a storage area shared only between the app and its own widget extension (an App Group).

This data never leaves your device except as the network requests to the public data sources below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Third-party data sources

To show live F1 information, the app fetches public data from:

  • Jolpica / Ergast-compatible API — schedule, standings, results.
  • Formula1.com public event tracker — session-time verification.

Like any internet request, these services receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address and request headers. We do not control these services; their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies. The app sends no personal information to them beyond what any HTTP request inherently includes.

Permissions

Android:

  • Internet / network state — fetch F1 data.
  • Foreground service, exact alarm, run at boot, wake lock — refresh home-screen widgets and tick countdowns on schedule.

iOS:

  • Background App Refresh — refresh widget data while the app is closed. You can turn it off in Settings; widgets then update when you open the app.

None of these access location, camera, contacts, microphone, photos, or personal files, and the app asks for no runtime permissions on either platform.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties. The app-store statistics above are used only for app improvement and maintenance.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data to us, please contact us immediately.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change to reflect it.
